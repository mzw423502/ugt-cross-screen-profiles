## Figure 1. Matching identities connects two assays to the complete reaction landscape

**A.** Y18 uses purified recombinant enzymes with individual acceptors; M25 uses recombinant lysate with 40-acceptor pools and LC–MS/MS. Matching enzyme sequences and acceptor connectivity gives 600 combinations, of which 578 were tested in both screens. **B.** Positive fractions for all 15 acceptors; black circles denote Y18 and open squares M25. Purple and teal labels identify hydroxycinnamates (HCA) and coumarins. **C.** The same 40 × 15 matrix is divided into two blocks of 20 enzymes for readability; column numbers refer to B. Colors and patterns distinguish shared positives, source-specific positives and negatives. Crossed white cells are the 22 Y18 untested units, excluded from binary denominators. M25 calls use cosine threshold 0.85. Acceptor matching is at connectivity level, with unresolved stereochemistry. Schematic and visualization code assisted by OpenAI Codex.

## Figure 2. Hydroxycinnamate contraction contrasts with coumarin reaction reassignment

**A.** Paired positive fractions for the six series members. Circles denote Y18 and open squares M25; each denominator is 39 except scopoletin (38). **B.** Decomposition of pairs positive in at least one screen. HCA has one shared positive and 37 Y18-only positives. Coumarins have 65 shared positives and 22 specific to each source despite identical totals of 87. Negative-pair counts are reported separately. **C.** Equal-enzyme changes and their difference D, with 95% family-prefix cluster-bootstrap intervals from 500 shared resamples per set. Filled diamonds show the primary set (39 enzymes, 17 clusters); the open diamond shows D for exact sequences (30 enzymes, 13 clusters). Δ is M25 minus Y18; D is ΔHCA minus Δcoumarin. **D.** Jaccard overlap of positive-enzyme sets among tested pairs. Supplementary Table S4 contains all 15 acceptors. Visualization code assisted by OpenAI Codex.

## Figure 3. UGT84A2 shows a selective cross-screen change within one enzyme

**A.** The six acceptor structures and paired reaction states for UGT84A2, grouped by chemical series. All three hydroxycinnamates are positive only in Y18, while esculetin and umbelliferone remain positive in both screens. Drawings show the recorded connectivity, without assigning stereochemistry or glycosylation sites; Me denotes methyl. **B.** Positive-acceptor counts for five enzymes, with the UGT84A2 row highlighted. The HCA and coumarin columns each contain three tested acceptors per enzyme. Circles denote Y18 and open squares M25; small vertical offsets keep identical counts visible. The other rows distinguish broader contraction from stable series profiles. Published biochemical evidence is summarized in Supplementary Table S14. Schematic and visualization code assisted by OpenAI Codex.
